## Packaging and promise

Title: Why Your Free Time Doesn't Feel Like Yours

Cover candidate: FINALLY FREE?

Concrete tension: an evening anticipated all day passes in unchosen scrolling while an intended activity remains untouched. Show a small voluntary alternative without promising a cure for exhaustion or claiming all phone use is harmful. The guitar is a story example, not a new productivity requirement.
