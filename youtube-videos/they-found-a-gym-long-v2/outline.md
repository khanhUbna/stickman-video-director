# Seven-part story structure
## Hook
Mara improves, then stops attending. The cancellation line promises an explanation.
## Open Loop
Vale wonders what happened to her. Three rocks outside the museum are planted as visible background information.
## Setup
apparent torture equipment, an open exit, voluntary payment. Mina tests Vale's assumptions.
## Build-up
reordering recordings by date changes the interpretation. Gradual load, recovery and aerobic work explain the equipment through evidence.
## Climax
identifying a gym still leaves the cancellation unexplained. A map and personal journal reveal a goal outside it.
## Payoff
the museum occupies the viewpoint Mara reached with Leo. Her later journal records continued activity outside the gym.
## Emotional Ending
Vale replaces his label, then takes a short walk before work. The unfinished lecture supplies one final joke.
