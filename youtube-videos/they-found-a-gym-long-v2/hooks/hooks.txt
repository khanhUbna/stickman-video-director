In the year three thousand twenty six, two archaeologists find an old membership file in a buried building. Its owner was Mara Ellis. Her records show progress. Then her visits stop.
They found a room where humans lifted heavy objects, put them back, and paid to return. Then one woman stopped coming.
You find a machine that makes people walk without going anywhere. Why would anyone pay to use it?
Mara kept getting stronger. Then she canceled her membership. A thousand years later, two archaeologists think they know why. They're wrong.
The archaeologists thought they'd found a torture chamber. Until they discovered the prisoners had paid to get in.
