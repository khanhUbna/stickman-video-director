A room full of heavy objects. A machine that takes people nowhere. And a receipt proving humans paid to come back.

In this fictional future-archaeology mystery, Dr. Vale and Mina uncover an ancient gym. But identifying the equipment doesn't explain why one member stopped coming just as her records showed progress.

An animated story about exercise, evidence, and the ordinary goals we can miss when we only look at the numbers. The characters, excavation, records and locations are fictional. The exercise explanations draw on public health and clinical guidance; individual needs, abilities and outcomes vary.
