# Courtroom outline

## Hook
Dirty bowl after two cycles; owner accuses, dishwasher counteraccuses. Money/time stakes and a specific complaint.

## Open Loop
Who caused the failed wash, and why does a long cycle not imply a faucet running all evening? Request replay of loading.

## Setup
Pre-rinsing footage and the unopened manual. Model-based scrape/pretreat distinction, no sensor-deception claim.

## Build-up
Active hydraulic cutaway answers water question; nested bowls and blocked arm become physically demonstrable obstacles.

## Climax
Just as machine appears to win, filter evidence interrupts. Inspection applies to the defendant as well.

## Payoff
Correct load, model-specific filter care, dishwasher detergent and proper cycle; fictional retest clears this bowl, without isolating a universal cause.

## Emotional Ending
Owner recognizes shared work. Machine lands one-line name joke. No extra lecture or subscribe request.
