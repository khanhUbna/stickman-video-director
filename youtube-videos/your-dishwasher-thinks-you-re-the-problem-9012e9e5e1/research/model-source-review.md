## Claims and exact evidence

### A. Do not pre-wash ordinary loosely attached soil; scrape
Full manual p16, saved TXT lines562–567:
`Do not pre-wash items with loosely attached food soil.`
`Remove all food particles, bones, toothpicks and excessive grease.`
`Pre-treat items having burned-on, baked-on or starchy soils.`

Quick start p1, TXT line2:
`IMPORTANT: For best cleaning results, do not rinse dishes before loading. Simply scrape off food remnants.`

Full manual p36, TXT lines1453–1461:
`The Bosch® filter system ... is designed to prevent the need to pre-rinse normally attached food soils from your dishes. Simply scrape leftover food soils off of dishware before loading. This process will conserve time, water and the energy ...`

Safe narration: For this Bosch example, scrape scraps rather than wash each normally soiled plate first. Exceptions: burned/baked/starchy soils may need pretreatment. Do NOT show whole bones, toothpicks, grease puddles entering pump. Do NOT claim 'leave every scrap on'.

### B. Do not block moving spray arms; no nested bowls
Full manual p16, TXT lines558–584:
`Ensure items do not protrude through the bottom of the racks and block the spray arms.`
`Load dishes ... so the insides of bowls, pots and pans are facing the spray arms.`
`Avoid nesting and contact points between dishes.`
`Use care to not block movement of the spray arms.`
`Only wash household dishware and kitchenware rated as dishwasher-safe.`
It also says push upper rack fully back so upper arm connects to rear water supply, and load pots/pans/bowls upside down on lower rack.

Safe animated conflict: nested bowls shield each other; a large pan handle obstructs the arm. The machine says 'I can't wash a surface the water can't reach' as a physical explanation, not a direct manual quotation. Show correct facing/space afterward. Do not use a picturesque loading arrangement that contradicts actual spray reach.

### C. Automatic soil sensing exists, no sensor-deception myth
Full manual p28, TXT lines1074–1076:
Auto: `Smartest cycle, with most flexible soil sensing, which is ideal for mixed dishware and food soils.`
Normal described as for a full load of normally soiled dishes. Full manual p30 discusses cycle times depending on soil and incoming water temperature.

Quick start p1, TXT lines54–56:
`Sensors in the dishwasher automatically increase the cycle time to ensure a good wash when heavy soil is detected.`

Safe narration: Its automatic programs can adjust to detected soil. No retrieved manufacturer evidence establishes that pre-rinsing necessarily tricks it into leaving dishes dirty. Do NOT assert 'the sensor needs food or it gets confused'. Pre-rinsing critique is wasted water/time and redundant work under recommended conditions, not guaranteed dirty dishes.

### D. Detergent only for dishwashers
Full manual p26, TXT lines963–979:
`Use only detergent specifically designed for dishwashers.`
`For best results, use fresh powdered dishwashing detergent or detergent tabs.`
`Do not use hand dishwashing products in your dishwasher.`
`Do not use too much detergent if your water is soft.`
It warns excess detergent in soft water may etch glassware/fine china. Amount depends on water hardness; avoid a universal dose or showing hand soap.

Safe visual: household hand-washing liquid tries to testify but is told it belongs at the sink; use dishwasher product label directions and model manual. Do not fabricate an explosive foam eruption as a guaranteed result; obvious comic exaggeration must remain recognizable.

### E. Filters and spray arms need care
Full manual p36, TXT lines1451–1490: cleaning need depends on usage habits and water hardness; visible retained soil/objects, lower performance or gritty film are reasons to inspect/clean. It supplies intervals for particular usage assumptions, which must not become a universal schedule.
Full manual p37, TXT lines1503–1545: remove filter as directed; rinse under running water with soft brush; no abrasive brushes/steel wool; reinstall and lock with arrows aligned. Check spray arms for grease/limescale and inspect deposits.

Safe narration: A dishwasher cleans dishes, not necessarily every trap or filter by itself; check its manual for filter and spray-arm care. This unit has a serviceable filter. Do not generalize manual-removable filters to all old/new units, or operate this example with filter removed.

## Rinse-cycle nuance
Full manual p28, TXT1093–1097: Rinse is for avoiding odors before a full wash; place unrinsed dishes inside and choose Rinse; stated as using less water than hand rinsing and very low energy.
Do not advise repeated sink rinsing while a load waits; do not claim all models have the same optional cycle. Burned/starchy pretreatment exception remains.
