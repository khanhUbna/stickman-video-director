Future archaeologists find a gym. One calls it a torture chamber and books a live demonstration.
Then his assistant finds the receipt.

An original animated science comedy about exercise, spectacularly bad conclusions, and the danger of writing the headline first. Dr. Vale has an audience waiting. Mina has evidence he would rather ignore.

All characters, discoveries, recordings and membership terms are fictional. The exercise explanations draw on the sources below. Individual needs, abilities and outcomes vary; a gym is one option for being active.
