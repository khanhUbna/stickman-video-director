# V3 — comic conflict rather than concealed identity
## Hook
Cold open: Vale is about to demonstrate an alleged torture machine live. He prepared the screaming; Mina runs in with actual instructions.
## Open Loop
Will Vale correct his spectacular public mistake? What is in the small back office and the tiny print on the card?
## Setup
Flash back two hours: excavation, heavy objects, premature classification, broadcast announcement. Mina explicitly offers training as an alternative; truth is not withheld from Vale or the audience.
## Build-up
Free exit and voluntary return challenge prison theory. A receipt escalates Vale's headline rather than instantly changing his mind. Same person's dated exercise records reveal adaptation. Treadmill paradox adds another concrete misunderstanding.
## Climax
Live demonstration: supervised slow walking gives Vale no catastrophe to perform. Robot lifts for him; a question makes the missing bodily stimulus obvious. He can stop the broadcast or acknowledge error publicly.
## Payoff
Outside-gym footage gives the effort practical meaning. Vale changes the sign on camera. Scientific payoff: repeated appropriately challenging activity can improve capacity, not one instant upgrade or pain for its own sake.
## Emotional Ending
Humility is expressed through a visible correction rather than a speech. A second comic payoff resolves the office and tiny-print clues: fictional cancellation bureaucracy gets the discarded torture sign. End on the changed label; no further moral, museum membership pitch or CTA.
