# Hook selection

Five candidates and actual heuristic results are in hooks.txt and checks/hooks.json.

Selected: Imagine this. You stop working, sit down, and hear someone coming. Why does resting for three minutes feel like getting caught? — 67/100, WORKABLE, automatic label The Mistake.

Alternate: You finally stop working. Why do three minutes on the couch feel like something you need to hide? — 68/100, WORKABLE, automatic label The Mistake.

The selected version wins editorially because the approaching person gives an observable trigger and a clean-plate callback. The independent reviewer recommended the brief Imagine this fiction cue, now adopted. These handcrafted scores are not predictions of retention, and the scene's three minutes is not a research statistic. No claim or number was invented to improve a score.
