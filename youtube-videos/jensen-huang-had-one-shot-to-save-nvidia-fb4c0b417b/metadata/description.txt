How did Jensen Huang keep NVIDIA alive when there was no room for another chip-design failure?
This animated business-history story explores the RIVA 128 gamble, early emulation and the cost of waiting.

Based on Huang's own retrospective account in his Acquired interview. A single narrator follows the architecture reset, the ICOS equipment purchase, testing before manufacture, direct production and the graphics functions the chip still lacked.

Alternative timelines are clearly hypothetical. This story does not establish that AI would not exist without NVIDIA, and it does not present reconstructed scenes as archival footage.

Primary source:
Acquired — NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang (October 16, 2023)
