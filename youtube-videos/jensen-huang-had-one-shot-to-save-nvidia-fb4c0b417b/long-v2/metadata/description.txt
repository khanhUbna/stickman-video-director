Jensen Huang had to rethink NVIDIA before the AI boom. What if the ordinary, careful chip-development process had exhausted the company's remaining resources?
This single-narrator animated history explores RIVA 128, emulation, missing graphics modes and the limits of counterfactual storytelling.

Based principally on Huang's October 16, 2023 Acquired interview. The six-month runway and same-day ICOS closure are his retrospective account, not independently audited here. A hardware review dated October 27, 1997 provides separate evidence of a later RIVA revision's strengths and limitations; it does not prove the original production decision.

Imagined alternatives are possibilities, not factual claims about what would have happened to NVIDIA or AI. No invented private dialogue or psychological diagnosis.
