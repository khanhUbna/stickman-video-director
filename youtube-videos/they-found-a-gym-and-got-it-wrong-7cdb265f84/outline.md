# Story outline

## Hook
Fictional year3026; heavy repeated lifting interpreted as torture. Water-bottle joke establishes confident expert and curious partner.

## Open Loop
Why do people return when they can leave? A photo in a bag is dismissed as irrelevant.

## Setup
Treadmill escape hypothesis, no restraints, breaks and familiar coach. Contracts/renewal overturn captivity interpretation.

## Build-up
Training records show manageable starting load, progression, rest and stopping pain; source-based physiology emerges from conflicting evidence.

## Climax
Fitness connects to ordinary life. The grandson photo becomes a personal training goal; this is a fictional illustration, not a clinical outcome claim. Museum label corrected.

## Payoff
An interactive museum lets visitors experience appropriately scaled replica equipment, with rest/instructions. The activity returns under another name.

## Emotional Ending
Returning visitors request monthly passes. Vale recognizes his own museum as a gym and reaches for staff registration. The expert becomes a participant; no second lecture or CTA.
