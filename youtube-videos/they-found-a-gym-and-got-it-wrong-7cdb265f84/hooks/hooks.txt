In the year three thousand twenty six, archaeologists uncover a room where humans lifted heavy things, put them down, and lifted them again. A torture chamber.
They found a room full of human suffering. Then they found the membership fees.
These archaeologists think they found a torture chamber. The people in the recordings kept coming back.
A man walks on a belt but never reaches the door. A future archaeologist thinks he knows why. He is wrong.
Why would humans pay to lift something, put it down, and lift it again? A museum is about to make the same mistake.
