# Research before drafting

New channel The Museum Got It Wrong: clearly fictional future archaeological comedy. Future year, people, recovered recordings, payment receipts, exercise logs, robot convenience and the later interactive museum are invented narrative evidence, not a historical record or a study. No copying of Motel of the Mysteries motel/toilet discoveries, characters or imagery.

Actual science to explain: resistance makes muscles work against force; repeated appropriately progressed training can increase strength. Aerobic effort increases breathing/heart activity; regular training can improve relevant system capacity/fitness. Overload/progression/specificity from HHS; changes accumulate over time, not after one difficult repetition. Recovery and appropriate form matter; pain is not a training goal. Useful movement can happen outside a gym, including walking, stairs or suitable bodyweight activity, not just memberships/machines. Match activities to abilities and don't imply a paid gym is necessary.

Narrative turn1: torture interpretation undermined by no restraints/open exits and records of voluntary payment. Turn2: effort is chosen to train capacities, with rest/progression. Twist: the future museum accidentally recreates a gym through hands-on exhibits and returning visitors, then sells its first monthly pass. A mirror of the premise rather than a second medical claim.

Careful list: no muscles-need-tearing myth; no lactic-acid soreness myth; no painmeansgains; no hormone/dopamine shortcuts; no claim all humans were inactive; comfort/automation in the depicted city is fiction, not documented universal causation. No exercise prescription, miraculous instant transformation or disability/age jokes. Fitness improvement isn't reduced to heavier weights alone. No numerical research findings or named published study invented. Institutional sources are guidance/synthesis, not lab findings.

Access notes: HHS PDF, CDC, Mayo and WHO retrieved; ACSM guessed page404 was excluded. Only source passages actually read support narration; source4 available but no unique factual claim uses it.
