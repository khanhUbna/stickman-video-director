Future archaeologists discover a gym—and mistake it for a torture chamber. Then they find the membership fees.

In this fictional animated mystery, a confident researcher and his curious partner follow moving belts, training logs and one overlooked photograph to understand why humans chose difficult exercise. The clues reveal resistance training, gradual adaptation, recovery and everyday uses of fitness. Their museum opening creates a problem neither expected.

The archaeological setting, characters, recordings and records are fictional. The exercise explanation draws on the sources below; results and appropriate activity vary by person. This is an educational story, not an individual exercise prescription. Physical activity can happen at home or outdoors, not just in a paid gym.
