# Draft action storyboard

Timings await accepted audio. Four-minute planning estimate only.

## Paragraph 1: Hook

Wide barbecue: four guests lift food; hero slaps own arm, spots three bumps. Snap cut to confused face.

Continuity/science: Burger stays on hero plate; hand withdraws after slap.

## Paragraph 2: Guest

Low tabletop track: female mosquito lands by burger, rejects it, takes off toward forearm.

Continuity/science: All six legs visible when landing; proboscis not a syringe.

## Paragraph 3: Breath

Hero exhales; stylized CO2 trail appears, mosquito turns toward its source. Hero starts to hold breath, shakes head and resumes.

Continuity/science: CO2 trail illustrative, not naturally visible; no advice to hold breath.

## Paragraph 4: Signals

Move from outdoor wide to mosquito viewpoint, warm body shapes and mixed odor traces overlay. Reveal both people breathing.

Continuity/science: Heat/odor cues overlap, no fixed sequential scanner.

## Paragraph 5: Chair

Hero grips chair, shifts it beside friend, sits; mosquito circles both, chooses hero; friend laughs.

Continuity/science: Chair moves before sit, planted feet, no sliding seated body.

## Paragraph 6: Lab

Match cut forearm to nylon sleeve; researcher removes cloth, labels two channels and releases mosquitoes.

Continuity/science: Show controlled odor apparatus, people not exposed to bites in this demonstration.

## Paragraph 7: Finding

Side-by-side actual-study inspired fabric samples; differing mosquito attraction; chemical magnification highlights several compounds.

Continuity/science: Name Aedes aegypti in small explanatory graphic, no invented counts/ratios.

## Paragraph 8: Limits

Hero briefly imagines industrial cleaner; red crossed-out idea; return to harmless mixed skin-odor dots.

Continuity/science: No product contact with skin, no acid treatment, one molecule never becomes sole cause.

## Paragraph 9: Myth

Friend imagines arm as candy; scene wipes to intact skin, mosquito making choice before contact.

Continuity/science: Sweet blood shown as character claim immediately challenged.

## Paragraph 10: Itch

Simple non-graphic skin cutaway: proboscis, saliva droplets, local response; two people have different visible reactions.

Continuity/science: No exact cellular sequence or scientific numeric bump tally invented.

## Paragraph 11: Eggs

Female carries supply crate toward eggs; male sips nectar, female nectar cameo; hero sees catering bill.

Continuity/science: Crate/bill obvious metaphors, not literal mosquito food storage.

## Paragraph 12: Protection

Hero puts on loose overshirt and applies registered repellent per label; friend stops laughing and reaches for bottle.

Continuity/science: No spray in eyes, mouth or under clothing; no brand or guarantee.

## Paragraph 13: Answer

Revisit two people with different odor blends in one wide frame; hero relaxes rather than looks ashamed.

Continuity/science: Do not depict odor as dirt or smell humans must notice.

## Paragraph 14: Ending

Hero takes burger; mosquito approaches covered sleeve and pauses, offended face; hold hero bite then cut.

Continuity/science: No promise repellent or cloth prevents all bites; no instant mosquito death.
