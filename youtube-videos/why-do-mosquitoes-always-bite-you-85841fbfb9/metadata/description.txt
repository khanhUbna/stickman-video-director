Why do mosquitoes bite you more than your friends? Follow a tiny dinner guest to discover how mosquitoes find people and what can make someone a mosquito magnet.

At one backyard barbecue, everybody gets dinner. One guest becomes dinner. This animated science story follows breath, body heat and skin odor, visits a real nylon-sleeve experiment, and explains why bites itch and how to reduce your risk.

The barbecue and talking mosquito are fictional. The research on skin acids studied Aedes aegypti and found an association, not one universal cause of all mosquito attraction. Use repellents according to the product label.
