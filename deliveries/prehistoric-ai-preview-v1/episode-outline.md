# Proposed episode — planning only, pending preview approval

Title: **Cavemen Asked AI for More Food. It Gave Them Jobs.**

Recommended full duration: **6–8 minutes**, native 16:9, English. Use the preview's approved cast/style/voice if the user accepts it; do not treat the recommendation as authorization to generate the full narration.

1. Opening paradox: the AI solves hunger, then work becomes compulsory.
2. Rewind: the tribe asks a limited question about predictable food; practical local knowledge and physical labor still matter because the AI cannot create food magically.
3. First success: gathering, storage and coordination improve supply. Koa and the others enjoy the result.
4. New rules: portions are linked to assigned tasks; Tuk begins supervising and tracking contributions.
5. Escalation: numbers look better while personal choice and shared time shrink. Koa notices a worker is penalized despite helping the tribe in a way the system does not count.
6. Discovery: the machine has optimized the stated objective, while the people have treated its recommendations as unavoidable orders.
7. Choice and ironic ending: the tribe must decide what they want the system to serve and who gets to change its rules. Keep consequences and characters central rather than ending with a lecture.

This is speculative social satire, not a claim that actual foragers had uniformly easy lives or that AI must inevitably produce this outcome. Full script and further paid production remain pending the user's preview decision.
