# Cavemen Asked AI for More Food. It Gave Them Jobs.

**18-second opening preview for user review**, English, native 16:9 / 1280 × 720 / 30 fps. Proposed full film length: **6–8 minutes**. The full episode has not been produced and must not be continued automatically before the user's decision on this exact preview.

## New prehistoric B6-look direction

Three new painted characters wear modest animal-hide loincloths, with white non-anatomical cartoon torsos, asymmetric meme heads, messy hair/topknot, simple dark stick limbs and stylized bare feet. Koa has a rust-red wrap and bone pendant, Tuk has a brown wrap and bone belt clasp, and Nim has a tan wrap. Modern shirts, trousers and rooms were replaced with a new painted savanna camp, hide huts, a serving stone, a carved work board and the deliberately futuristic cyan-eyed AI artifact.

The story is a fictional Stone Age/AI thought experiment. It is not presented as a factual reconstruction of prehistoric societies.

## Acting and source

Koa walks to the AI and gestures from his stomach toward its eye. A later shot shows the supervisor offer meat: approach, shared grip, release and the receiver drawing it toward himself. After the next time cut, Tuk blocks another reach, Koa recoils on fixed feet and changes expression, and the camera reveals Nim performing a supported backswing/downstroke with a stone axe. The log visibly separates at the strike. The supervisor's bone pointer keeps a fixed length while rotating toward the work rule.

`rig.py` is the existing project-local articulated extension refitted to the new alpha-backed head/body assets. Source shoulder outlets were measured on each new body. Head variants retain the same neck mounting and width; only front-view assets are used. No invented continuous turn or nonuniform actor scaling is claimed. The installed B6 trusted compositor remains untouched; its standing-only reports and formal 35-subcheck reference acceptance are not attributed to this preview.

Generated atlases are separated into sprites for video compositing. Arm/leg artwork is rendered as single owned dark chains, with the relevant forearm intentionally behind held meat and the hand exposed at the actual bone grip. One world-space meat transform drives the shared handoff; no duplicate independent food follows each person.

## Voice, sound and cost

Exactly one `tts-1`/`alloy` request generated 10.4125 seconds of new native-speed speech. It is saved and reused. An opening offset, two explicitly authored silent holds and the final nonverbal action produce the 18-second picture. Offline PocketSphinx aligned the actual words; no paid transcription or repeated voice request was made.

Speech cost estimate: **USD 0.002535**, based on the previously documented USD 15/million-character rate, not independently verified account billing. Three image requests supplied the character atlas, camp and Koa expression variants. The image tool did not expose a monetary cost, so none is invented. The third facial-variant request was announced before execution. Music, outdoor bed, AI reply, footsteps, arm swishes, pointer and axe/wood Foley are original local synthesis.

## Reproduce locally without API calls

Requires Python 3.10+, NumPy, Pillow and FFmpeg/FFprobe. PocketSphinx is only needed to redo alignment. The saved speech, aligned words and generated artwork are included.

```bash
python timeline.py
python film.py
python finish.py
```

Do not rerun paid art or speech generation for an animation correction. The source archive excludes reproducible intermediate movie/audio files, retaining source art, actual voice, aligned words, font/license and the actual motion/technical report.
