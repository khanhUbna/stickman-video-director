What if AI arrived before civilization?

Koa only wanted dinner. A strange machine helps his tribe find more food, waste less, and finally rest. Then someone invents a score.

Soon, every basket earns a mark, every meal requires proof, and the person keeping the food safe has the lowest score of all. When a storm hits, the tribe discovers what their new system was actually measuring.

An original animated story from AI Arrived Too Early: impossible technology, very familiar human problems.

This is fictional prehistoric satire, not a historical account. Narration and artwork were created with AI tools; story, animation, editing, original music and sound design were produced for this episode.

If the machine saved you two hours a day, who would decide what those hours were for?
