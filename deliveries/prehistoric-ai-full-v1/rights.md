# Production provenance and permitted reuse

- Script and staging: original fictional story authored for this project. No reference-channel subtitles are reused in the narration.
- Cast and base camp: reused from the user-reviewed `prehistoric-ai-preview-v1` production, generated specifically for this channel.
- Additional art: four image-generation calls recorded in `art/generation-ledger.json`. Three painted environment plates and one transparent expression/AI asset atlas. No external image search assets.
- Narration: saved OpenAI `tts-1` / `alloy` output. New chapter files and request/voice ledgers are preserved; the preview narration is reused without a new request.
- Music: original deterministic local plucked-tone/percussion arrangement implemented in `sound.py`. No third-party song, music library license or Content ID clearance is being represented.
- Foley and rain/current: original local filtered noise and tone synthesis tied to visible actions and recorded foot contacts.
- Font: Archivo Black, SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `fonts/OFL-ArchivoBlack.txt`.
- Code: MIT, see `LICENSE`.

There is no external music track requiring attribution. No claim is made that automated rights checks can guarantee a platform will never flag a generated or synthesized asset. No YouTube publication was performed.
