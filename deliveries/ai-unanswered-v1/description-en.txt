AI can give you an answer. But how would it know?

Koa's machine helped him find food and prepare for winter. Then he asked three questions that needed more than a confident reply:

Are we alone in the universe?
Can a machine actually feel anything?
What should a life be for?

Follow one caveman from a star-filled ridge to the warmth of a cave and a choice beside the river. Along the way, an empty cup, a moving shadow and a very helpful machine reveal three different kinds of not knowing.

This is not a claim that AI will never answer difficult questions. It is an original animated story about evidence, experience, values, and the difference between a fluent answer and a justified one.

Which question would you ask AI—and what would convince you that its answer was true?
