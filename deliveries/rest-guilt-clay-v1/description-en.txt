Why do you feel guilty when you rest—even after the urgent work is done? This animated story explores rest guilt, productivity guilt, and why it can feel so hard to relax.

You finally sit down to watch a movie. One email turns into another. Your break ends before it begins. Then you hear keys at the door… and suddenly you are washing a plate that was already clean.

Through one fictional evening, we explore the pressure to earn a break, the kindness you offer a friend but struggle to give yourself, and one small choice that changes the ending. The story draws on research about leisure beliefs and conditional self-worth; it does not claim there is one universal cause of guilt.
