Why does your dishwasher leave dishes dirty? In this animated courtroom comedy, a frustrated owner accuses his machine of wasting water and failing its one job. The dishwasher requests a replay.

Follow the evidence through pre-rinsing, nested bowls, blocked spray arms and a removable filter. A long cycle does not mean a faucet running the whole time, and one dirty bowl does not prove either the owner or the machine is always at fault.

Objects on Trial turns everyday frustrations into funny, evidence-led science stories.

The courtroom, characters and clean retest are fictional illustrations. Loading and filter-care demonstrations are based on the Bosch SHEM63W55N/11 family manual; follow your own appliance instructions. Stubborn and starchy residues may need pretreatment, and genuine faults can require service.
