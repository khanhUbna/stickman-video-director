# Why You Avoid the Work That Matters Most

Why do you procrastinate on work you genuinely care about? You can care deeply about a project and still avoid opening the folder. Follow Maya as a portfolio application turns into kitchen cleaning, easy emails and one more productivity app—and see what changes when she makes the next action concrete.

This illustrated story explores short-term emotional relief, different kinds of friction, a rough first draft and a plan for returning after a setback. Maya is fictional. The exercises are experiments, not a diagnosis or a promised cure.
