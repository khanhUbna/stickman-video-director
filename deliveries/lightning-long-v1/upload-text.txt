TITLE
Lightning Hits the Ground. Then What?

DESCRIPTION
The flash disappears. But what happened beneath your feet?

Follow Bolt and Ground through a storm's hidden story: current spreading through soil, sand that can turn into glass, a cow with four very inconvenient contact points, and a car whose tires are taking too much credit.

A funny animated explanation of ground current, step voltage, fulgurites and why a closed metal vehicle can offer protection.

The diagrams are simplified and slowed down, not safe-distance maps or outdoor experiments. Hear thunder? Seek proper shelter. Wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before heading back outside.
