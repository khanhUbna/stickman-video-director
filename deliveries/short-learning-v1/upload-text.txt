TITLE
Why You Forget What You Just Learned

ALTERNATIVE TITLE
You Watched It. So Why Can't You Do It?

DESCRIPTION
You watched every step. It all made sense. Then you tried it yourself… and got stuck.

Recognizing a step and doing it are different things. Try one small move without the tutorial, check what you missed, then try again.

A short animated story about the gap between feeling like you've learned something and being able to use it.

What tutorial made you think “That looks easy”—until you tried?
