# One Small Break — 15-second clay motion trial

Purpose: demonstrate an actual articulated 3D character engine on Codex Cloud, independently of the B6 renderer. This is a movement trial, not the unapproved full Psychology Facts episode.

- Canvas: 1920 × 1080; encoded rate 30 fps; duration 15.000 s.
- Character posing: deterministic 12 Hz, with precise hand/prop ownership and planted stance feet.
- Look: matte clay, roughness 0.94, seeded bump texture, warm cream room, coral sofa, sage accents and a green waistcoat.
- Face: gray clay hair, separate pupils/brows, blink and a smile after the catch.
- Cameras: fixed wide setup, pickup view, toss view with extra headroom, then return to wide. Cuts, not a continuously orbiting/zooming camera.
- Assets: original procedural meshes/textures/sound only. No generated external images, HeyGen service or music catalog.

| Seconds | Action and visible result |
| --- | --- |
| 0.00–0.60 | Character notices the orange ball; head and body prepare. |
| 0.60–3.85 | Six alternating footsteps move across the set; the nonmoving foot stays anchored. Arms counter-swing. |
| 3.85–5.65 | Turn, bend hips/knees and reach to the ball. The prop stays on the floor until the grasp. |
| 5.65–6.65 | Stand and lift the ball with the right hand. |
| 6.65–8.15 | Inspect the prop, turn the head and gesture with the free arm. |
| 8.15–9.90 | Anticipation, release at 8.65 s, airborne arc clear of the head, catch at 9.90 s. |
| 9.90–10.60 | Knees absorb the catch; expression changes toward a smile. |
| 10.60–13.10 | Walk toward the sofa while holding the prop. |
| 13.10–15.00 | Sit on the cushion, lower the prop into the lap and settle. |

Timestamps describe the authored action, not speech or subtitles. No new voiceover was requested. Original local foley/notes are synchronized to footsteps, the lift, release, catch and settle.
